Deep Shelter Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

Deep Shelter is a mobile game made by an independent developer (published on Google Play as "thasri"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: your game stays on your phone, we have no servers, and we don't collect your personal information. The game's ads (optional reward videos and an occasional full-screen ad between dives) are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show them (see Advertising).

Information the game stores

Your game progress (your shelter, crew, items and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features, and it does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Your payment details are given to Google, never to us. The game receives only a confirmation of the purchase (the item and an order reference) so it can deliver what you bought. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Game engine (Unity)

Deep Shelter is built with the Unity game engine. Unity may automatically collect limited technical information about your device, such as the device model, operating system version and app performance, to keep the engine working well. This is described in Unity's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The game shows two kinds of ads: optional reward videos, where you choose to watch a short ad in exchange for in-game coins, pearls or help, and now and then a short full-screen ad between dives. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), information about your device, and how you interact with the ads. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where consent applies, you can change your choice in the game at any time under Settings > Ads and privacy. Players who buy "No Ads" never see an ad: their rewards come without a video.

If online features are added in a future update, this policy will be updated first to explain what changes.

Children

Deep Shelter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not collect it ourselves. The only data that leaves the game is what Google Play (for purchases) and Google AdMob (for ads) process, as described above.

Your choices

You can delete all game data at any time by uninstalling the game or by clearing its storage in your phone's settings. Purchases you made stay in your Google Play account history, managed by Google.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
